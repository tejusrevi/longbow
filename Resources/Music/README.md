# Music

Drop the two loops here (any of .m4a, .mp3, .wav, .caf, .aac):

- `menu_music.m4a` — title screen, online lobby, toss and results
- `duel_music.m4a` — during a match

They loop seamlessly and crossfade when you move between a match and the
menus. Missing files are fine: the game just plays without music.
Keep each under ~3 MB (AAC at 128 kbps is plenty).
